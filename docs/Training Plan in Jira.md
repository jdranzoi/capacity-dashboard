# Training Plan in Jira

## **General Objective**

This training plan aims to organize and manage the training process within the company efficiently. Using Jira's functionalities, we ensure that all team members receive the training they need. We build our training structure using epics, releases, components, and labels. This creates a clear tracking system and gives us an efficient way to allocate resources without redundancy and to add more efficient redundancies when necessary.


Live MCT board uses **parent / sub-task** person-year plans (`[Name] - YYYY Training & Development Plan`) rather than Jira Epics. Dashboard grain is the child activity. Releases = Fix versions, then Components, then Labels.

### Epics

  
Epics in Jira represent each individual involved in the training plan. Each epic is associated with a specific person and groups the various trainings (tickets) that the person needs to complete. The *reporter* for each epic should be the person responsible for it within the company, making it easy to identify who to contact for any situation.

The objective of epics is to centralize and organize the trainings for each team member in one place. This allows for personalized tracking and ensures that each person has a clear and specific training path, avoiding overload or incorrect training assignments.


### Releases

*Releases* are used to classify and group trainings according to specific areas of knowledge or skills. The release groups include categories such as Soft Skills, Hard Skills, CRM, AI, iPass, CMS, ECP, Cloud, Search Engines, Analytics, and Headless.

The objective of releases is to segment the training plan into thematic blocks that facilitate tracking progress in each area. This ensures that the trainings are aligned with the company's strategic goals and that progress in key areas can be measured.


### Components

*Components* allows for more specific tracking within each release. These may include technologies, platforms, or specific roles such as Kibo, Jitterbit, Boomi, BigCommerce, AWS, QA, TL, PM, FED, FSD, among others.

Each component will have a designated point of contact, who is responsible for overseeing and identifying new training opportunities in that area

The objective of components is to add an additional level of detail to the tracking of trainings, allowing one or more components to be associated with each ticket. This facilitates the identification of specific knowledge or roles within each training area and helps to avoid duplication of efforts, ensuring that the trainings are relevant and applicable.


### Labels

*Labels* are used to refine and specify details within a component. They apply to very specific aspects being reviewed in a ticket, such as NextJs, React, BC Catalyst, AWS Cloud Practitioner, among others.

The objective of labels is to provide an additional layer of precision in the categorization of trainings. This allows filtering and searching for tickets by very specific technologies or topics, ensuring that the training is highly relevant and targeted to particular areas of interest or need.
